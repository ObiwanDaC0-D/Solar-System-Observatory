Data and accuracy

Planetary and lunar positions are calculated locally with Astronomy Engine (VSOP87/ELP-based analytical astronomy). This is not a live telescope feed. Rise/set calculations use geometric altitude crossing at 0°, except the Sun, which uses −0.833° to approximate the visible solar limb and refraction. Results are approximate and can differ from observed times due to terrain, elevation, weather, and atmospheric refraction. Times are shown in the browser's local time zone, not automatically the time zone of manually entered coordinates. An internet connection is needed to load the astronomy library. Do not use for navigation or safety-critical decisions.
